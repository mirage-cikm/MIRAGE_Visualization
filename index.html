Methods & notes

  • Human baseline: World Values Survey Wave 7. Means are official country sample means; entropy is estimated from reported response proportions (Shannon, natural log) — the same convention as the paper.
  • LLM data: zero-shot persona injection, temperature 0.3, 100 personas/country (the paper’s confirmatory study uses 10,000/country for 8 countries). Means and entropy computed directly from model responses.
  • Models: paper models — gpt-4o-mini, gemini-2.0-flash, deepseek-chat; extended — gpt-4o, gemini-2.5-flash, deepseek-reasoner.
  • Categories (7): the paper’s 23-item → 7-category mapping — Dishonesty & Corruption (5), Sexual Ethics (4), Life Ethics (5), Political Violence (2), Surveillance & Privacy (3), Family & Relationships (3), Moral Philosophy (1).
  • This is an exploratory tool. Formal significance testing — paired t-tests, Friedman tests, signed Cohen’s dz, and Bonferroni correction — is reported in the paper, not here.
  • Relation to the paper: for the eight countries analyzed in the paper, the confirmatory values (10,000 personas/country) are reported in the paper’s Figures 2–3; values shown here (100 personas/country) are exploratory and may differ slightly.
  • Missing country–item cells (not in WVS Wave 7) are shown in grey.
